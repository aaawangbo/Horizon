# Evaluation goal

Evaluate whether this item deserves attention from a Chinese AI blogger who wants timely, accurate, useful, and differentiated topics for a broad technology audience.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Must cover.** A major model or product launch, credible breakthrough, large platform or policy shift, serious incident, or unusually important open-source release with wide audience impact.
- **7-8: Strong candidate.** Timely and well-supported news with a clear practical consequence, useful technical insight, strong discussion value, or a distinctive content angle.
- **5-6: Optional.** Relevant but incremental, narrow, repetitive, weakly sourced, or unlikely to support more than a short mention.
- **3-4: Low value.** Routine updates, familiar opinions, shallow summaries, promotional framing, or claims with little concrete evidence.
- **0-2: Noise.** Spam, off-topic material, ungrounded rumor, clickbait, duplicate content, or fabricated claims.

# Evaluation guidance

Consider timeliness, audience relevance, novelty, source quality, practical impact, technical substance, discussion potential, and whether the item supports a useful original angle. Prefer primary sources and concrete details. Penalize hype, missing evidence, recycled announcements, speculation presented as fact, and minor version bumps without user-visible consequences. Popularity alone is not importance.

Use three to five specific topic tags. The reason should state both the strongest value signal and the most important caveat.
