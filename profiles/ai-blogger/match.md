# AI blogger radar profile

Use this profile for timely, evidence-backed developments that can become useful Chinese-language AI content.

In scope:

- major model, product, API, agent, multimodal, hardware, and platform releases;
- useful open-source AI tools, repositories, benchmarks, and engineering practices;
- credible research results with practical or industry implications;
- pricing, policy, safety, copyright, security, and ecosystem changes that affect AI users or builders;
- practitioner analysis and community discussion that adds concrete evidence, limitations, or counterexamples.

Out of scope:

- rumors without a primary or independently credible source;
- routine patch releases with no meaningful user impact;
- generic tutorials, recycled explainers, SEO pages, affiliate promotions, and exaggerated predictions;
- stories that merely mention AI but contain no substantive AI development;
- duplicate coverage that adds no new facts or interpretation.
